# 005 — Doping Silicon

Pure silicon is useful, but it becomes far more useful when engineers deliberately change its electrical behavior.

## What Doping Means

Doping means adding a very small amount of another element to silicon.

The added atoms change how charge can move through the material.

## N-Type Silicon

N-type silicon has extra available electrons. Those electrons can move and carry charge.

## P-Type Silicon

P-type silicon has places where an electron is missing. These vacancies are called **holes** and can behave like positive charge carriers.

## Why Both Types Matter

When P-type and N-type materials are placed together, their boundary creates useful electrical behavior. Current can be encouraged, blocked, or controlled depending on how voltage is applied.

That gives engineers the raw structure needed to build diodes and transistors.

## Connection to the Next Layer

Doping does not create intelligence. It creates regions whose electrical behavior can be controlled. By arranging those regions carefully, we can build a transistor: a switch controlled by another electrical signal.
