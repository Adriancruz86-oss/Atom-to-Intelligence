## Build Strategy

Each major concept should eventually include:

1. A plain-language chapter.
2. A diagram or system map.
3. A small demonstration or lab where useful.
4. A connection to the next layer.
5. A short knowledge check.

The roadmap is intentionally ambitious. The project is designed to grow over years rather than be rushed into a shallow finished product.
